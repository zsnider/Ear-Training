# Ear Training
Audio ear training games with custom settings. Train your ear.
Upload your own music or audio loops to play the games. Or use pink noise by default. 

When adding your own folder of music, I recommend adding a smaller folder at first to avoid long upload times. Try ~10-20 tracks at first. Any audio file should work (.mp3, .wav, etc.). 

Loops and one-shots included in this repository are limited and may take up to 30 seconds to load on each game. 

1. EQ Match. Match an equalizer to an unknown equalization. Fully customizable. 
To begin, try enabling only the peaking filter, set the db tier to 10-12 db, boost only gain direction, and set the Hz frequency range to something your headphones or speakers can actually play. Pink noise is great to understand the tonality of different frequencies. 

2. Compressor. Match to unknown compression settings. Fully customizable.
To begin, try just adjusting the make-up gain and one setting at a time (custom mode). Try to hear the difference when only adjusting the threshold or ratio. 
   
3. Reverb Master. Match to unknown reverb settings. 

4. Distortion Master. Match to unknown distortion/saturation settings.

7. Freq Quiz. Guess the frequency at which audio is boosted or cut.
Simpler than EQ match. A quick game to rapidly get acquainted with the frequency spectrum. 

8. Spatial Specialist. 2 games in one. Guess audio panning or width.
A quick, simple game. Under panning mode, one audio source is panned. Under width mode, two audio sources are panned left and right. 

9. Quick Compress. 2 audio signals. One dry. One compressed. Guess which.
Intended to be a quicker, simpler game than "Compressor". Just click the compressed audio.
