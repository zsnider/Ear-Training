# Ear Training
Audio ear training games with custom settings. Train your ear.
Upload your own music or audio loops to play the games. Or use pink noise by default. I recommend adding a smaller folder at first to avoid long upload times. Try ~10-20 tracks at first. Any audio file should work (.mp3, .wav, etc.)

EQ Match. Match an equalizer to an unknown equalization. Fully customizable.

Compressor. Match to unknown compression settings. Fully customizable. 

Reverb Master. Match to unknown reverb settings. 

Distortion Master. Match to unknown distortion/saturation settings. 

Freq Hunter. Audio is bandpass filtered and panned. Guess the location.

Freq Quiz. Simpler than EQ match. Guess the frequency at which audio is boosted or cut.

Spatial Specialist. 2 games in one. Guess audio panning or width.
