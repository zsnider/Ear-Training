Where to start

By default, only Delay Time is active to guess — Feedback and Wet/Dry are fixed and shown. Press New Round, toggle between Target and Your Delay, then adjust the Delay Time slider until the repeat interval matches. Press Submit when you're ready.

Unlock Feedback and Wet/Dry using the lock buttons to add more parameters. Increase difficulty to push the parameter ranges further apart, making each round more challenging.

What to listen for

Delay Time — the gap between the original sound and its first echo. Short delay times (under 100ms) produce a slapback or doubling effect; longer times (300ms+) produce distinct, clearly separated repeats. Try tapping along to the rhythm of the repeats to estimate the tempo.

Feedback — how much of the delayed signal is fed back into the delay line, creating multiple repeats. Low feedback (20–30%) gives one or two echoes that fade quickly. High feedback (80%+) creates many repeats that build and sustain, almost like a wash of sound.

Wet/Dry — the balance between the delayed signal and the dry original. High wet values make the echoes prominent and forward in the mix. Low wet values keep the original sound upfront with echoes subtly trailing behind.

A useful trick for Delay Time: listen for the moment the first repeat arrives after the original sound, and count how long that gap feels. Short = fast tempo or slapback; long = spacious echo. Use Space to A/B between the Target and Your Delay to hear how close you are, and D to check the dry signal and re-anchor your sense of the original.
