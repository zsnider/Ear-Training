Interactive games to sharpen your listening skills — EQ, compression, dynamics, reverb, distortion, delay, and spatial perception. Built for producers, engineers, and curious musicians.

The Games

↗
EQ Match
A hidden EQ curve is applied to audio. Drag your bands to match it by ear — frequency, gain, and Q.
Frequency Gain Q / Resonance 6 Filter Types
↗
Compressor
A target compressor is applied to audio. Dial in threshold, ratio, attack, release, and knee to match it.
Threshold Ratio Attack / Release Knee
↗
Freq Quiz
A frequency is boosted or cut. Listen and pick the right frequency from 9 choices — fast, multiple choice ear training.
Hz Boost / Cut Multiple Choice Quick Rounds
↗
Level Logic
A reference signal plays, then a level-adjusted version. Identify whether the gain was boosted or cut, and by how much.
Gain Boost / Cut dB Recognition Quick Rounds
↗
QuickEQ
Two EQ curves are shown — one is applied to the audio. A/B listen and identify which curve matches the target by ear.
A/B Identification 5 Filter Types 1–3 Bands Quick Rounds
↗
Quick Compress
Two signals play side by side — one compressed, one dry. Pick which is which before time runs out.
A/B Identification Compressed vs Dry Ratio Quick Rounds
↗
Freq Hunter
A bandpass-filtered sound plays at a hidden stereo position. Click the 2D grid to identify both the frequency and pan.
Frequency Stereo Pan 2D Grid Spatial Hearing
↗
Spatial Specialist
2 games in 1! In panning mode, a sound plays at a hidden stereo position. In width mode, guess the stereo width.
Stereo Pan Width Mono Quick Rounds
↗
Signal Chain Architect
A hidden signal chain processes the audio. Listen and arrange the effects — EQ, compression, reverb, saturation — in the correct order.
Signal Flow Order Recognition Drag & Drop 4 Effect Types
↗
Reverb Master
A hidden reverb is applied to audio. Match the room size, decay, pre-delay, damping, and wet/dry by ear.
Room Size Decay Pre-Delay Wet / Dry
↗
Distortion Master
A hidden distortion is applied to audio. Match the drive, type, mix, and tone by ear.
Drive Soft / Hard Clip Tape Tone
↗
Delay Master
Match a target delay by ear. Set delay time, feedback, and wet/dry to replicate the processed signal. Lock any parameters for hints.
Delay Time Feedback Wet / Dry A/B Compare
