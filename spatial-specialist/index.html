Where to start

Start in Panning mode. Press Play and listen to where the sound sits in the stereo field. Drag the handle on the stereo ruler to your best guess of the panning position, then press Submit.

Use headphones if possible — stereo imaging is far more precise and reliable through headphones than speakers, especially for subtle positions near center.

What to listen for

Panning — listen for the balance between your left and right ear. A fully panned signal arrives entirely from one side. Center sounds equally loud on both. Intermediate positions feel weighted toward one side but not exclusively.

Width — rather than a single point in space, a wide signal fills the stereo field with a sense of "spread." Listen for how much of the left-right space the sound occupies. A mono signal collapses to a single point at center; a wide signal feels expansive and enveloping.

Trust your first instinct. Listening too long causes your brain to adapt and the position becomes harder to judge. Toggle the dry signal with Space/D as a reference point to re-anchor your perception.
